   Agreement contains no meaningful predictive information.

4. **No agreement category shows consistent positive EV across all pairs.**
5. **Pairwise agreement (15min×4h agree: +0.02 pips) is marginally positive but negligible.**
6. **1h×4h agreement is actually negative (-0.16 pips), further undermining MTF signal designs.**

### RECOMMENDATION

**Cross-timeframe agreement does NOT contain meaningful predictive information.**
The hypothesis that timeframe agreement improves signal quality is **not supported**.
