## Minimum Methodological Adjustment

The data is structurally insufficient. Three options, in order of preference:

### Option A: Wait (Recommended)

**Wait for more data.** The runner is producing 6 bars/day. To reach the minimum for GARCH(1,1) on all pairs:

| Milestone | Bars/pair | Calendar days from now | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| GARCH minimum | 60 | ~26 days | ~2026-10-12 |
| Horizon 12 feasible | 42 | ~1 day | ~2026-09-17 |
| Horizon 18 feasible | 48 | ~2 days | ~2026-09-18 |
| Horizon 24 feasible | 54 | ~3 days | ~2026-09-19 |
| Robust GARCH | 100 | ~11 days | ~2026-09-27 |
| Multi-regime (200+) | 200 | ~28 days | ~2026-10-14 |

**Within 3 days** (by 2026-09-19), horizons 6–24 will all be feasible. **Within ~26 days**, GARCH estimation will be reliable.

### Option B: Reduce Scope

Run R2.1 with only:
- Models: Naive, RollingVol, EWMA (GARCH-free)
- Horizons: 6 only
- Evaluation: 4–5 walk-forward steps
- Interpretation: Preliminary signal detection only, no statistical claims

This is honest but produces very weak evidence.

### Option C: Pool Across Pairs

If pairs share volatility dynamics (they don't — each currency pair has unique properties), pooling could increase sample size. **This violates the R2.1 design** because pairs are not exchangeable.

---

## Recommendation

**STOP. Wait for data.** The runner is healthy and producing bars. Within 3 days, horizons 6–24 become feasible. Within ~26 days, GARCH estimation becomes reliable.

Do not run an underpowered experiment. Do not reinterpret criteria after seeing results. The data will be sufficient soon — patience is the minimum methodological adjustment.
